Cookie Policy

Understand how and why we use cookies on the Living Dairies website.

Last Updated: January 1, 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile phone) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, remember your preferences, and provide information to website owners about how their site is being used.

Cookies do not contain any personally identifiable information on their own, but they may be linked to personal data we hold about you in accordance with our Privacy Policy.

2. How We Use Cookies

Living Dairies uses cookies to:

  • Ensure the website functions correctly and securely.
  • Remember your preferences and settings between visits.
  • Understand how visitors navigate and use our website.
  • Measure the effectiveness of our content and marketing.
  • Improve the overall user experience on our website.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

Cookie Type Purpose Duration
Strictly Necessary Essential for the website to function. These cannot be disabled. They include session management and security cookies. Session
Functional Remember your preferences such as language, region, and form inputs to provide a more personalised experience. Up to 1 year
Analytics Help us understand how visitors interact with our website by collecting anonymous data on pages visited, time spent, and navigation paths. Up to 2 years
Marketing Used to track visitors across websites to display relevant advertisements. Only activated with your explicit consent. Up to 1 year

4. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies on our website are set by third-party services we use, including:

  • Google Analytics: To analyse website traffic and user behaviour anonymously.
  • Google Maps: Embedded on our Contact page to display our farm location.
  • Google Fonts: To load custom typography used across the website.

These third parties have their own privacy and cookie policies, which we encourage you to review. We do not control the cookies set by these services.

5. Managing & Disabling Cookies

You have the right to accept or decline non-essential cookies. You can manage your cookie preferences in the following ways:

  • Browser Settings: Most browsers allow you to view, manage, block, and delete cookies through their settings menu. Refer to your browser's help documentation for instructions.
  • Opt-Out Tools: You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of our website. Strictly necessary cookies cannot be disabled as they are essential for the site to operate.

6. Cookie Consent

When you first visit our website, you will be presented with a cookie consent notice. By clicking "Accept All", you consent to the use of all cookies described in this policy. You may also choose to accept only strictly necessary cookies.

You can withdraw or change your consent at any time by clearing your browser cookies and revisiting our website.

7. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, legislation, or our business practices. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised date. We recommend checking this page periodically.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about our use of cookies, please contact us at privacy@livingdairies.pk or visit our Contact page.
