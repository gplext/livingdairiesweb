02

Where the
good milk comes from.

At livingdairies.com, we don't just sell milk — we raise it. Our farm in the lush plains of Punjab is home to over two hundred well-fed, grass-grazed cows, tended to by a team that treats them like family.

Every morning before sunrise, we hand-collect the milk, chill it within minutes, and dispatch it to your home — no preservatives, no powders, no pretense. Just real, pure milk the way it was meant to be.

  • High quality natural feed, hormone-free cows
  • Hand-collected before sunrise
  • Chilled within 15 minutes of milking
  • Delivered same day — never older than 12 hours
