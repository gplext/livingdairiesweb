Message from the Board
"

At Living Dairies, we believe that the health of a nation begins with the purity of its food. Since our founding in 2008, we have committed every sunrise and every harvest to delivering dairy products that are not merely fresh — but alive with nutrition, care, and integrity.

Our herd of over 230 high quality frezian breed cows locally raised feed with high quality & specially selected feed certified by our nutritionist, and tended by a team of over 15 dedicated professionals who share one mission: excellence from pasture to package. We do not cut corners. We do not compromise on quality. We invest in the best technology, the cleanest facilities, and the healthiest animals — because you and your family deserve nothing less.

As we grow, we carry the same founding promise forward: transparency, sustainability, and an unwavering respect for the land that feeds us. Thank you for trusting Living Dairies with your family's nourishment.
