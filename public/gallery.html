Dawn at the Farm

The farm comes alive at 4:30 AM as the first milking shift begins. Morning light filters through the sheds as our team begins the day's work.

Livestock Sheds

Our 12 climate-controlled sheds offer optimal ventilation, hygiene, and comfort for every animal regardless of season or weather.

Free-Roaming Enclosures

Our cows enjoy natural movement and grazing in expansive areas, promoting their physical well-being and natural herd behavior.

Pasture Expansion Reserve

Lush green reserved areas preserved for optional grazing or growing animal feed. The soil is fertilized and watered to ensure the pasture land remains sustainable.

Tags Monitoring

Each cow wears a smart tags that tracks health vitals, milk production, activity, and reproductive cycles in real-time via our farm dashboard.

Milk Quality Testing

Every batch of milk is tested for fat content, protein levels, bacterial count, and antibiotic residue before it leaves our plant.

Cold Storage

A 1,200 sq/ft refrigerated warehouse maintains products at a constant 4°C from post-processing until delivery, guaranteeing freshness.

Shed Electricity System

We have a reliable electricity supply system that ensures all sheds are well-lit and operational throughout the day.
